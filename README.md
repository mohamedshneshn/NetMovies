# NetMovies

**NetMovies** is a Salesforce-based movie-tracking and recommendation application that enables users to view their movie history, provide feedback, and receive personalized movie suggestions based on their viewing patterns. The application uses Salesforce's powerful data management tools to track customer interactions and offer tailored recommendations.

## Project Overview

The **NetMovies** app is designed to provide a seamless and engaging experience for movie enthusiasts. It tracks the movies a customer has watched, collects feedback on those movies, and uses that feedback to recommend new movies they may enjoy. The app will also generate insightful reports and dashboards for both users and administrators to analyze trends and improve the recommendation system.

### Key Features:

- **Movie Tracking**: Track movies that customers have watched and their feedback.
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Offer movie suggestions based on viewing patterns and ratings.
- **Feedback System**: Allow customers to provide feedback on movies they have watched.
- **Reports & Dashboards**: Provide analytical insights on movie preferences and trends.
- **Customer History**: A complete history of movies viewed by the user, along with feedback and ratings.
